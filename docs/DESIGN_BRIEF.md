# Design Brief

## Direction
Create a distinctive premium botanical/editorial identity rather than a generic ecommerce template. Use the existing Tablog design work as a quality and usability reference, not as a layout to copy.

## Visual language
- Warm ivory and soft neutral surfaces.
- Deep botanical green as the primary action colour.
- Muted sage as a supporting accent.
- Elegant serif display typography paired with a highly legible sans-serif.
- Generous whitespace, strong hierarchy and restrained borders.
- High-quality, truthful product photography; no fabricated packaging or clinical imagery.
- Subtle, purposeful motion that never blocks content or harms performance.

## Experience priorities
1. Mobile-first product discovery and checkout.
2. Clear price, availability, delivery and ordering information.
3. Accessible contrast, focus states and form validation.
4. Fast-loading imagery with responsive sizes and meaningful alt text.
5. Calm, clear payment and order states.
6. Dashboard density appropriate for daily seller operations.

## Content constraints
- Do not invent ingredients, certifications, testimonials, clinical evidence, treatment outcomes or dosage.
- Do not repeat the flyer’s infection-treatment claims as established facts.
- Product imagery must accurately represent the actual product once the seller supplies approved photographs.
- Use neutral placeholder copy only during development and label it as placeholder.
